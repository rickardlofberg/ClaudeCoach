## How the last 9.5 weeks went

**Phase 3 exit (Jun 17 – Jul 1, as planned):** Went well. The very-light recovery week and the rebuild week both landed close to plan, capped by a genuinely easy 27 km long run on Jun 29 (avg HR 133, no drift) — the "canary" session the Jun 17 review was watching for, and it passed clean.

**Phase 4, the July disruption (~Jul 3–29):** Turned out more disrupted than the season plan's active/passive fork anticipated on the light side — a dancing weekend followed by a full week of illness (no running at all, Jul 20–26) sat inside the already-flagged Jul 7–21 travel/holiday window. Total running Jul 13–29 was three short runs across 17 days plus the blank week. This matches the season plan's own contingency ("if fully passive... week 1 of the build needs to be a rebuild week, not the 28 km long run").

**Phase 5, the ultra build (nominally Jul 22 – Aug 31):** Started about 12 days late in real terms — the first build-volume long run was Aug 3, not Jul 22 — for the reason above. Rather than accept the shortened runway, the last three weeks (Aug 3–23) were run harder than the season plan's own table to make up the lost time:

| Week | Season plan target | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Aug 3 wk (plan wk1) | 50 km, 28 km long | 63.3 km |
| Aug 10 wk (plan wk3, B2B 28+20) | 58 km | 60.3 km, no B2B — isolated long runs instead |
| Aug 17 wk (plan wk5, B2B 42+28) | 65 km | 74.5 km — but as two races + an unplanned 42 km, not a designed B2B |

The planned back-to-back long-run weekends — the core specificity stimulus of Phase 5 — never happened as designed. What happened instead, Aug 15–23, was heavier and less controlled:

- **Aug 15 — Ultravasan 45**, 44 km ultra race run deliberately as a controlled long effort (suffer 572, the biggest single session on record). HR discipline held even as pace faded — a genuinely good signal for Sörmlands pacing.
- **Aug 19 — Convinistraffen**, a hard 5 km Kundo effort (avg HR 163, suffer 75), 4 days after Ultravasan 45.
- **Aug 22 — Stockholm Trail 21K**, a technical race that ran harder than expected (suffer 352), 7 days after Ultravasan 45.
- **Aug 23 — an unplanned 42 km run**, intended as ~25 km, extended by getting lost, the day immediately after the 21K race. Raw HR zones read "easy," but `characterize-activity` found genuine aerobic decoupling (HR climbing 130→139 at flat pace) — a real fatigue event, not a clean easy day. The athlete's own Strava caption calls it "kind of dumb ahead of Sörmlands 100."

That's four hard-to-huge efforts in 9 days, 3–4 weeks before the A-race.

**Recurring minor pattern, unresolved:** across late June and July, several runs logged as plain "easy" (Jun 18, 21, 28, 30; Jul 10, 19, 29) actually sat at avg HR 150–157 against a <145 target. Asked about it directly — no clear cause identified; the stated intent throughout was to keep HR down. Given no explanation surfaced and it didn't recur as sharply in August, this is noted rather than acted on. **Decision:** during the taper, run by feel/conversational effort as the primary gauge rather than chasing the HR number if the two disagree.

## Discrepancies and decisions

- **July disruption was fully passive** (dancing weekend + a sick week, zero running Jul 20–26). **Decision:** already reflected in a delayed, compressed build — no further action, just context for why the build got compressed and pushed.

- **Deliberate catch-up push, Aug 3–23, went past sustainable.** After the disruption, training was intentionally increased to recover lost build time. Result: two races 7 days apart plus an unplanned marathon-distance run the day after the second one, no injury but general tiredness and soreness now. **Decision (user's own call, confirmed today):** stop pushing. Taper starts now, 19 days before Sep 12, rather than continuing one more nominal build week as the original season plan's Phase 5 Week 6 prescribed.

- **No designed B2B long-run weekends happened.** The season plan named these as the most important sessions in the build. **Decision:** accept it — the two races plus the accidental Aug 22→23 back-to-back arguably delivered an equivalent (if uncontrolled) specificity stimulus, and Ultravasan 45's HR discipline at 44 km is a genuinely useful pacing data point for race day. Not worth trying to retrofit a "real" B2B into a taper window; that would just add more load on top of an already-acknowledged edge-of-sustainable block.

- **"Easy" runs running hot through June–July**, no clear cause found. **Decision:** don't chase this further right now; use feel over HR number during the taper (see above).

- **No injuries reported.** Tired and sore, no structural pain flagged. **Decision:** Week 1 of the taper (below) is a genuine recovery week, not just a volume step-down, given the acute fatigue.

## What changes from the prior plan, and why

The season plan's Phase 5/6 boundary (one more build week Aug 25–31, then an 11-day taper Sep 1–12) is replaced: **the taper starts today, Aug 24, for a full 19 days.** Reason: the July disruption compressed the build, the response was to push harder than planned for three weeks to compensate, and that push landed exactly where a first-time-100km athlete doesn't want to be three weeks out — tired, sore, and by the athlete's own assessment "on the edge of what is sustainable." Extending the taper banks the fitness already built (including two real races and a huge, well-paced 44 km effort) rather than risking it for marginal extra volume. The B2B specificity sessions the original plan called for are accepted as not having happened in their designed form; the actual load from Aug 15–23, while uncontrolled, likely served a comparable purpose.

Sörmlands 100 (Sep 12) is confirmed as still the goal — no change to the season plan's A-race.
