- **DXT date — June 14, not June 12.** The race was always on Saturday the 14th; the May 31 plan had it wrong. **Decision:** No consequence to training — the approach was right regardless of which day.

- **DXT harder than planned.** "Hardest race this year so far." Sustained sub-threshold effort for 3+ hours on recovering marathon legs. Quads still loaded 3 days later. **Decision:** Phase 3 recovery timeline is extended to respect this reality. First quality session remains off the table until the ultra build (Jul 22). The first week back should be genuinely light.
