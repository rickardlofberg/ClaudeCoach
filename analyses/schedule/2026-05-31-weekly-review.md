Avg HR 163.9 is consistent with a disciplined effort rather than a blow-up; the body hit a first-marathon wall, not a pacing error. No injuries. No knee escalation.

First-marathon fitness context: 1:40:20 HM (Apr 25) projects ~3:29–3:35 all-out in ideal conditions. 3:50 in the heat on a first marathon is a reasonable outcome — the gap is experience and conditions, not base fitness.

- **Marathon result — 3:50:27 vs 3:38–3:43 target.** Warm conditions + first marathon + tired legs in the final 10 km. Avg HR 163.9 consistent with a disciplined effort. **Decision:** Not a fitness verdict — conditions and first-marathon experience account for the gap. The base is there for a sub-3:45 in better conditions. No changes to the season plan needed.

### DXT strategy (Jun 12)

You'll be 13 days post-marathon. The legs will not be recovered. That's accepted and already baked into the season plan.

- **Hike all uphills.** Don't try to run them — save the quads for the descents and flats.
- **The descents will hurt.** First-marathon quads are the most sore at downhill impact. Go slower on the descents than feels necessary.
- **No time target.** Finish, eat the aid station food, enjoy the Dolomites.
- **DNF over injury.** Sörmlands 100 is Sep 12. A twisted ankle or tendon flare in the Dolomites costs 4–6 weeks of the ultra build. This race is C-priority.
- **Gear:** Check the mandatory kit list before packing. Poles useful if allowed.

## What I'm watching for

- **RHR Jun 1–5.** First-marathon recovery is often slower than runners expect. The benchmark is: back within 5 bpm of normal by Jun 5–6. If it's still elevated on Jun 6–7, drop the Jun 5–6 runs and arrive at DXT on less running (that's fine).
- **Knee on first run back (Jun 3).** 42 km loads the knee differently than long training runs. Note if it's louder than pre-marathon — if it is, shorten Jun 5–6 and flag it before the ultra build.
- **DXT quads (Jun 12).** The limiting factor will be eccentric quad loading on the descents. Manage pace downhill proactively.
- **Jun 6 canary.** If 12–14 km feels awful and HR is drifting high, the recovery is still in progress — move DXT into "walk + short run" mode and accept it, rather than forcing a jog that sets back the Jul–Aug build.
- **Post-DXT fatigue.** After Jun 12, two hard events in 13 days means the next weekly review (around Jun 13–14) determines when the ultra build actually starts. Be patient with it.
