## Macro phases

### Phase 1 — Marathon taper (May 12–29, 18 days)
Last big long run is done (32 km on May 10). Now it's about staying sharp without adding fatigue. Volume drops ~15–20% each week. One quality session in week 2, light strides in race week. Detailed day-by-day in `analyses/2026-05-12-stockholm-marathon-block.md`.

### Phase 2 — Post-marathon recovery + DXT (May 31 – Jun 12, 13 days)
Marathon demands 10–14 days real recovery minimum. Given DXT is on day 13, training is walks + very easy running only. Goal is to arrive at DXT with the legs moving, not to chase fitness. Detailed schedule in `analyses/2026-05-12-stockholm-marathon-block.md`.

### Phase 3 — Full recovery from two-race block (Jun 13 – Jul 6, ~3.5 weeks)
Two hard efforts in 13 days. Recovery debt is larger than either event alone. Weeks 1–2: easy aerobic only, no targets, no quality. Week 3: back to easy 40 km/week with the first medium-long run.

### Phase 4 — July disruption (approx. Jul 7–21, ~2 weeks)
Known travel/holiday. Keep running by feel — volume as logistically possible, no required quality, no long-run targets. Goal is to keep the aerobic base ticking without fighting the schedule. Don't try to make up missed work; just stay moving.

### Phase 5 — Ultra build (Jul 22 – Aug 31, 6 weeks)
This is the heart of the Sörmlands prep. Six weeks is tight for a first 100 km, but the current base is solid and the goal is to finish — not to race. The build structure:

The B2B weekends are the most important sessions of the whole plan. Running on tired legs from the previous day is the core ultra-specific stimulus — it teaches pacing, nutrition, and mental management better than any single long run.

**Quality during the build:** Keep the Tuesday Kundo sessions, but pivot the intent. Intervals are less valuable than in marathon prep — the body needs aerobic volume. Threshold or easy-with-strides is preferred over hard 4×4 VO2 in weeks 3–6.

**Trail specificity:** At least one run per week should be on trails by August. If possible, reconnoitre Sörmlands course terrain (forest, rolling, rooty) rather than road long runs.

### Phase 6 — Taper (Sep 1–12, 11 days)
Short taper — ultras need less taper than marathons. Volume −40% in week Sep 1–7, then very light Sep 8–11. Intensity preserved: a couple short strides each week to stay sharp. Legs should feel restless by race morning.

## Training style: polarized

Most volume at HR <145 (easy/conversational). Tuesday Kundo group session is the one quality anchor per week. The long run is the main aerobic stress, not a second quality session.

**Why polarized:**
- Matches the existing weekly rhythm (one quality + one long) — nothing needs inventing.
- Lowest injury risk for ultra training volume, which matters for the knee.
- Sörmlands 100 is finished on aerobic engine and legs, not VO2 ceiling — the long easy miles are the training.

**Practical rules:**
- Easy runs genuinely easy: HR <145, conversational, no watching pace.
- If Tuesday group is doing hard intervals and the plan calls for easy: run easy alongside them or skip the interval reps.
- The long run is easy unless the plan explicitly calls for a marathon-pace finish segment (Phase 1 only).

## What I'm watching for

1. **Knee escalation.** Currently background noise. If it starts affecting gait, or if 15+ km runs start provoking it, flag immediately and adjust — losing 1 week to a prevention protocol beats losing 3 weeks mid-build.
2. **Marathon recovery curve.** RHR 3–5 days post-marathon is the indicator. Still elevated at day 7 = recovery is slower than expected; push all subsequent sessions back 3–4 days.
3. **B2B weekend quality.** The key question in August is not "did I do the km?" but "did the second day feel sustainable?" If the Sunday run after a big Saturday is a death march, reduce Saturday's volume, not Sunday's. The goal is arriving at Sörmlands knowing your legs can still move on day 2.
4. **July disruption actual shape.** If July is more active (hiking, lots of movement) the build can accelerate in late July. If it's fully passive (beach, sitting), week 1 of the build needs to be a rebuild week, not the 28 km long run.
5. **Pacing and nutrition rehearsal before Sörmlands.** By Aug 25, Rickard should have a clear nutrition plan (gels/real food ratios, hourly calorie target) and have tested it on at least one 3+ hour run. First-ultra failures are often GI, not legs.
