—— SEASON JOURNAL · MAY 12 → SEP 12 ——

Nineteen days between now and the start line.

The season built toward one goal: finish Sörmlands 100, a first ultra, on Sep 12. It didn’t arrive on the original script — a marathon ran hotter than planned, DXT turned out to be the hardest race of the year, July went fully passive, and the ultra build was compressed into three weeks that were pushed past sustainable to make up lost time. The taper starting today banks the fitness already built — including a 44 km controlled ultra effort with real HR discipline — rather than risking it for one more nominal build week.

AFTER THE FINISH LINE

The first hundred. Then decide.

Nothing is scheduled after Sörmlands. The season plan says it plainly: decide after the race how the body feels and what Q4 looks like. A first 100 km finish — however it goes — earns real recovery before any new target gets named.

Two open items carry into race week regardless of outcome: a short Sörmlands race-day plan (aid stations, gear, pacing off the Ultravasan 45 data) was flagged in the season plan for mid-August and never formally written — the two hard races and the Aug 23 long run likely rehearsed fueling anyway, but nothing is on paper. And resting HR is still the May estimate of 48 bpm, never recalibrated against real morning readings.
